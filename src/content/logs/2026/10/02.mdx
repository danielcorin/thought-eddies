The AI labs need to make trillions in revenue to support today's debt commitments.

There are different ways to use AI to help with your work. One is to use it to do the work you were already doing pre-AI:

“Check the spreadsheet to see how much revenue we made this quarter.”

“Email Jane with the findings from our investigation.”

A more interesting way to use AI is to build the tools and systems that empower you to do your job effectively, or even change the work you spend your time doing.

Instead of prompting your agent to read a spreadsheet, build the one-pane-of-glass tool that gives summary statistics on important revenue goals.

Instead of having the agent write about your investigation findings, have it build an interactive HTML report you can send to your colleagues so they can see not only your findings but also the thought process that got you there.

I think of this approach as building cognitive scaffolding rather than offloading thinking to an agent. And you should verify that the tools you build work correctly, just as you verify that you built your spreadsheets properly, so you can trust what they are telling you.

I don't know if using AI like this will pay the bills for the big labs, but this is the way I see AI transforming work.
